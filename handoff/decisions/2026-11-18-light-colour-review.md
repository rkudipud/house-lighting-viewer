# Lighting colour proposal

**Review proposal; homeowner confirmation and product selection pending. Not for construction.**

The homeowner prefers whitish HIGH light and yellowish LOW light. The coordinator selected
4000 K for the living, dining, master-bedroom and children's-bedroom HIGH mains, subject
to homeowner review. The previous setting was 3000 K.

The existing 2700 K coves, LINE and mood strips and the 3000 K reading and art lights
remain unchanged. Fixture positions, circuits and scheduled lumens are unchanged. The
base schedule has 30 fittings at 2700 K, 12 at 3000 K and 62 at 4000 K.

The linked viewer, schedules, drawings, BoM and prior simulation outputs predate this
proposal and have not yet been regenerated. Their 3000 K mains specifications should
not be treated as the new proposal. Saved lux estimates are not measured results.
