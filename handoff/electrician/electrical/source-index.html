Request the exact strip, power supply, controller and repeater as one documented, sample-tested proposal. Quantities remain subject to site measurement.

Controls and electrical reference documents

  • Skydance T1-K manual: candidate single-colour, 12-48 V controller, three 4 A outputs, maximum 12.5 A input, 2/8 kHz selection. It states higher frequency reduces output current. The project screens at 2 kHz and 2.4 A/output as a planning assumption, not a manufacturer-certified rating at site temperature. The precise repeater, strip, branch distribution and protection remain unverified. The manual's illustrated connections do not certify the complete project chain.
  • Mean Well HLG-480H-24 datasheet, p. 8: reference family has 24 V, 20 A and 480 W nameplate; at above 230 V AC, its graph shows 100% load to 60 C ambient, versus 50 C at 110 V AC, reducing to about 60% at 65 C. The static graph shows 80% at 90 V AC and 100% from 110 V AC. The stated maximum case temperature is 90 C. Verify combined input/thermal conditions and mounted tc with the manufacturer; these graphs are not the project 0.60 assumption. The 480 W class is a homeowner sizing preference, not an approved manufacturer, suffix, order code or installation. Its nameplate output current exceeds the candidate T1-K input rating; protective coordination and all downstream ratings must be designed.
  • Mean Well ELG-240 datasheet: the 24 V model is rated 240 W at 200-305 V AC, but 180 W at 100-180 V AC. The living strip's 181.52 W nominal load alone exceeds 180 W, before any losses. Do not use 240 W nameplate as a universal low-line capacity.
  • Mean Well LED-driver installation manual and the bundled lookup conflict with the HLG-480H datasheet on B16/C16 maximum counts. The datasheet states 2/3 units; the retained installation-table lookup has 1/2. Neither number is a mixed-circuit or actual-breaker approval; resolve with the manufacturer and electrician.
  • Legrand Lyncus 677304: manufacturer India listing describes 3-50 W and up to 8 LED lamps; supporting table wording is inconsistent. Actual driver input watts, count, ambient and edge require written clarification. A nominal W comparison is not a compatibility test.
  • Legrand Arteor Advance AA24107MB technical sheet: LED 7-200 VA, maximum 10 lamps, and +5 to +35 C operating range for the documented models. India product listing. The 35 C operating limit matters for local wall-box assessment. VA is not watts.
  • Schneider AvatarOn E8331RD250_WE India page and instruction sheet: documented dimmable-LED 3-100 VA. Exact edge, Indian stock and compatible driver remain unverified. An assumed power factor cannot establish the real VA.

All ten mains-dimmer groups, including the 48 V track's upstream control, remain on procurement hold. C37 is not cleared by the CBR position decision. Do not use a fan regulator, an undocumented phase-cut dimmer, or a dummy load to disguise incompatible equipment.
