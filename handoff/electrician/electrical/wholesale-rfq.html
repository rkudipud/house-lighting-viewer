Send these questions and require an itemised answer

  1. What are the manufacturer, importer, exact orderable SKU and variant, current datasheet and manufacturing batch? Is it available in the requested quantities to the Hyderabad delivery postcode, and what is the lead time?
  2. Provide source CCT/bin and tolerance, lumens and optical distribution, power at measured input voltage, CRI and R9 reports. For strips, give emitted lm/m, profile-delivered lm/m, PCB width, cut increment, reel length, maximum run, mounting/profile needs, temperature limits and permitted feed/injection method. Identify the test lab and applicable test method; do not infer R9 from CRI.
  3. Give actual cutout diameter, body depth, mounting spring clearance, bezel/trim, profile cross-section, diffuser, cable exits and accessible driver dimensions. The project does not yet know the site dimensions: do not substitute a guessed standard diameter or pocket width.
  4. Name every supplied LED module, driver/CV supply, wall dimmer, repeater and accessory SKU. Provide written manufacturer approval for the entire exact chain, mode, frequency, number of units and loading, and the specified power-failure memory. Quote suitable overcurrent/branch protection and installation guidance for the electrician to review.
  5. Provide full ambient and case-temperature derating, input range, surge/inrush, power factor/VA, start-up and low-end dimming performance; confirm replaceable parts and whether visible flicker, hum, strobing, ghosting and cold-start problems are within agreed limits.
  6. For bathrooms/balcony/utility, provide complete-assembly IP/IK, wet-zone and connector evidence and cleaning/chemical/UV limits. Ask the electrician to assess the actual zone and current standards.
  7. Give unit price, per-flat and four-flat totals, GST separately, shipping, labour, profiles, drivers, controls, accessories, cut-waste, installation, after-sales terms and exclusions. If catalogues show MRP, distinguish dated MRP from a binding delivered quote.
  8. State warranty duration and terms, Hyderabad service address, repair/return logistics, availability of replacement drivers and matching CCT bins, spare costs and discontinuation support. Imported stock is acceptable only if its exact specification, service and replacement commitments are evidenced; do not assume it is inherently compatible or durable.

Sample and acceptance

Before any purchase or construction release, the supplier and qualified electrician should bench-test the exact proposed group: low-to-high smooth dimming, intended minimum and maximum group counts, all six living modeled outputs, repeater tracking, dimmed start, OFF glow, mains-loss memory, actual strip current, colour/bin matching, fan/camera flicker, hum, temperatures and measured voltage drop. Mock up the actual curtain pocket, seating sightlines and profile/track clearance. Record product codes, measurements, failures and agreed replacements; retain the sample as the comparison standard.

The homeowner may choose appearance and approve a budget, but a supplier statement, visual sample or manufacturer family is not a substitute for electrical design, exact-chain evidence, site measurements or a separate construction release.
