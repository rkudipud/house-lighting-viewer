**Notes**
- **IS grades:** BWP is a grade within IS 303:2024 (72-hour boil). "Marine" is IS 710:2024, which adds glue-shear minimums (avg ≥1,000 N, none <800 N after boil), mandatory preservative treatment and no extender in the glue (PlyPrice BWP-vs-marine article, Nov 2026, secondary). The same article says the IS 303:2024 testing plan has **no termite/borer test**. I did not read the IS text itself.
- **HDHMR:** PlyPrice says there is no separate Indian Standard for HDHMR, so ask for the brand's density and swelling data. Action TESA's Boilo board is >1,000 kg/m³, BWP and IS 5509 fire-retardant (₹113/sq ft for 8 mm).
- **Aluminium moisture/termite claims:** These come only from marketing. Elite Aluminium (Hyderabad, 2026-06) says "Moisture & Termite Resistant" with a 10-year warranty. The Coimbatore blog says "termite proof, fire resistant, 15–25 year life". Neither is a lab spec. The Elite page body showed no price, so the "from ₹500/sq ft" in its search title is a snippet only.
- **HomeLane Hyderabad page:** It shows no prices. It lists moisture-resistant ply, MDF, "Hydroguard Plus" and hardware from Hettich, Hafele, Ebco and Blum.
- **Turnkey spread:** F33 and F34 differ about 2–3× for plywood wardrobes. The bases are likely different (finish, loft, floor-to-ceiling, hardware), and neither source defines the sq ft.
- **Bed envelope:** The 1.68 × 2.13 m envelope minus a 1.524 × 1.981 m mattress leaves 156 mm of width and 149 mm of length (my arithmetic) for the frame. Real frame dimensions are unverified.
